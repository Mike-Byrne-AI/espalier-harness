The SessionStart banner OPENS the session with Claude's own *provisional
first-thoughts read* of the continuity (blueprint + GOAL + Notes-to-next-session)
— reasoning **over** the handoff, not a passive status dump that restates it.

**Why (design bet, not a measured result).** Self-generated thinking about
injected context is more load-bearing than the passive injection alone: the model
attends to its own committed reasoning differently than to handed-over text, and
reasoning over the context forces *integration* (chain-of-thought applied to
orientation) instead of leaving it as unintegrated tokens. It also surfaces the
read so the operator can correct it on turn one.

**Calibration.** Mechanism is sound; **magnitude is UNMEASURED** — a cheap,
plausible bet, not a proven uplift. Do not oversell it.

**The provisional framing is load-bearing, not decoration.** Self-generated
reasoning anchors in BOTH directions: a confident *wrong* first read primes the
whole session with the error (generated errors anchor harder than passive context
the model might have ignored). So the open MUST be framed as a hypothesis
("…confirm or redirect?"), with the operator-in-the-loop as the corrective — the
make-it-prove-it / fast-earns-scrutiny ethos pointed at orientation itself.
