| `DEF-3084` | tests/_box_lock.py::BoxLock.release (and scripts/guard_cost_census.py::BoxLock.release) | **The box lock logs each acquire and no release, so how long the lock is held, and so how full a box is, cannot be read from its log.** `tests/_box_lock.py::log_outcome` is called when a run takes the lock (`taken`, or `waited` with the wait's length and the holder it waited behind), passes through inside its holder (`inside`), gives up past its bound (`gave_up`) or goes unguarded; `BoxLock.release`, called at `pytest_unconfigure`, truncates the holder record and unlocks, and writes no line. The guard cost census keeps its own copy of the holder (`scripts/guard_cost_census.py::BoxLock`, pinned to the plugin by `tests/test_guard_cost_census.py::TestTheHolderIsTheBoxLocksShape`), appends to the same log, and its `release` writes none either. Drive, on the win box's live log (`~/.espalier/espalier-box.lock.log`, 2026-11-15T09:23Z): 2174 lines, 1437 `taken`, 712 `waited`, 25 `gave_up`, and no line naming a release. A hold's length is in the log only where a waiter stood behind it, and then as the wait, not the hold. Who is hurt: the operator deciding whether a box can take another seat, who gets wait times (0.5 to 2.2 h a day on the Air over a week, air's read of 2026-11-14) and no utilization: more seats on a box add queue, and this log is how that queue would be measured. The operator ruled the row filed on 2026-11-15 in the dispatcher window (air's assign 20261115T065041Z-air-aa9da0). Fix shape, inside the lock's own module and its census copy: one `released` line per hold, written at release while the lock is still held, so in the log a hold's release precedes the next run's acquire and lines pair in file order; it carries `held_s` (the hold's length, from the take) with `start` at the take; the census's copy the same, held to the plugin's shape by its pin. A holder that is killed writes no release line (the kernel frees the lock with the process), so its acquire stands alone and its hold's end is bounded only by the next acquire: a declared limit of the log, not of the fix. The probe drives both copies in-process on a temp lock and prints the smaller of their counts of `released` lines: 0 while either writes none. | minor | HYGIENE | OPERATOR |
