| `DEF-1132` | tools/cc/hooks/write_guard.py::_secret_read_targets_one (the read loop's redirect break, which ends the operands at a < as at a >) | **The Bash secret-read leg never reads an input redirect's source, so `cat < .env`, the most natural spelling after `cat .env`, reads a dotenv the guard allows.** `tools/cc/hooks/write_guard.py::_secret_read_targets_one` stops collecting operands at the first redirect word (`raw.startswith(">") or raw in ("<", ">")`), which is right for an output redirect, whose target is a write, and wrong for `<`, whose operand is exactly what the verb reads.
