Measured 2026-10-18 on the first cut of the recall engine's comment strip (the failure-mode review): its code-span regex, a lazy repeat of `(?:(?!\n[ \t]*\n).)`, passed the gate while a crafted 45 KB paragraph with 99 backtick-run lengths took 6.3 s and an 86 KB one 84 s through `tools/cc/hooks/_recall.py`'s tokenizer, which SessionStart's corpus walk runs over every indexed document; the lane bounded that regex, so no shipped pattern carries the shape today. Who is hurt: the maintainer who adds such a pattern to a hook and reads the gate's green as a ReDoS review, and an adopter whose declared recall source then carries the input that stalls it. Fix shape: the detector reads the pattern's structure (an unbounded repeat over a group whose body can match any character, through `sre_parse` or a scan for a group holding a bare dot followed by `*` or `+`) and flags it; a test that trips on the shape above; the allowlist re-reviewed against the widened detector.
