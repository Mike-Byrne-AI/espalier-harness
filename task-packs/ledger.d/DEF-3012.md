| `DEF-3012` | tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS_WRAPPER (lowercase and bare); tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS_WRAP_RUN (every command-position consumer) | **The command-position wrapper words are read lowercase and bare, so a wrapper spelled in another case or with its `.exe` hides the command behind it, on the Bash tool and in every PowerShell reader that steps over the roster.** `tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS_WRAPPER` spells `env`, `sudo`, `nohup`, `timeout` and the rest in lowercase with no suffix, while `DEF-1161` made every binary verb case-blind with an optional `.exe` (`tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_EXE_SUFFIX`), and Git Bash runs the other spellings as the plain words (the host behaviour that row drove for rm). Driven in-process 2026-10-16 on the Bash tool: `ENV cp /tmp/x <hook>`, `NOHUP tee -a <hook>`, `nohup.exe tee -a <hook>` and `Sudo cp /tmp/x <hook>` draw no zone deny (4 of 4; each plain spelling denied), and `env.exe rm -rf <checkout>` draws no wall. Who is hurt: the `DEF-1161` user, a Windows operator in Git Bash whose agent spells a wrapper as it spells the binary: a write into the harness or a delete of the checkout behind it meets no tier. Fix shape: spell the binary wrapper words case-blind with `_EXE_SUFFIX` and keep `command`, `exec` and `time` (builtins and a keyword) case-sensitive, as `DEF-1161` kept the directory and loop readers; `tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS_WRAP_RUN` fronts every command-position consumer, so its ReDoS budget (`tests/test_redos.py::test_cmd_pos_linear_on_repeated_prefix_run`, which runs on POSIX only) is the oracle the change needs, and the wrapper roster's own pins read the new spelling. Found by the failure-mode review of the lane that closed `DEF-1161`, `DEF-1162` and `DEF-1174` (2026-10-16), and left out of that lane because the budget cannot run on the Windows host that drove it.
