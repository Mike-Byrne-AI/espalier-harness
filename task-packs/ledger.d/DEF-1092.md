Found by the 2026-10-03 adoption audit (`espalier-issues/audits/2026-10-03-adoption/public_report.md`, B-1); re-checked at `08ab2db`: PARTLY-FIXED: ownership reporting became marker-aware (`73050c2`, DEF-957 closed): `ownership_summary` now splits `.claude/` paths into `marked_under_claude` and `adopter_owned_under_claude`, and doctor treats an unmarked file as the adopter's. Who is hurt: an adopter who already had a `.claude/` harness and must work out by hand which of two overlapping commands or gates wins.
