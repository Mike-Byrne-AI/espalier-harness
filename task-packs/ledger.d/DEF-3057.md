| `DEF-3057` | tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS (an unquoted expansion that may be empty hides the verb) | **An unquoted expansion that may be empty, standing at a command position, hides the verb after it: `$U mv cc/x /tmp/y` and `sudo ${U} mv cc/x /tmp/y` are allowed, where bash with `U` empty (or unset) runs `mv cc/x /tmp/y`.** No arm of `tools/cc/hooks/_bash_patterns.py::_CMD_POS` reads a bare parameter expansion, a command substitution or a backtick span as a word that can vanish: at the anchor's start the verb prefix takes `$` and then needs the verb, and behind a wrapper the run (`::_CMD_POS_WRAP_RUN`) stops at the word. Base walled the braced spellings only because the closing `}` of `${U}` read as a statement boundary; the DEF-3043/3044/3053 lane made a brace a separator only at a word's start, which moved 55 such rows deny->allow (11 env-prefix, 44 zone), accepted by the dispatcher as an explicit trade (ack 1a9932 (2), ruling request 1bb010); the plain `$U` spelling allows on base too (driven), so the gap is every command-position reader's, not the wrapper run's alone. Who is hurt: an operator whose agent runs a command built from a variable that is empty in this shell (`$PREFIX mv cc/x /tmp/y`, `sudo ${SUDO_ARGS} rm -rf tools/cc`; judged, never run here), whose zone write or delete is let through. Fix shape: the anchor treats an unquoted `$NAME`, `${...}`, `$(...)` or backtick word at a command position (bare or behind a wrapper) as one that may be empty, so the word after it is read as a command position too, with its floods in `tests/test_redos.py` and walls for `$U`, `${U}`, `$(true)` and the backtick beside each bare twin. The probe drives the hook on the two rows above and counts the allows: 2 while the defect stands. | minor | LOGIC_BUG | ADOPTER |
