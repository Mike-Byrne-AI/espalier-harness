Driven at HEAD on the real renderers: an edit to `tools/cc/hooks/write_guard.py` fires the advisory; an edit to `espalier/_vendor/cc/hooks/write_guard.py` fires nothing from any renderer; the same edit under `espalier/_vendor/selfcheck_tests/` or `espalier/assets/claude/` fires a discard warning. Who: the maintainer who edits the vendored copy and gets no signal that the next sync overwrites it from `tools/cc/`.
