Two shipped bodies then call the per-machine blueprint durable: `espalier/assets/docs/WORKFLOW.md` (`Records durable reasoning (decisions, rejected alternatives...) into the cognitive blueprint`) and `espalier/assets/claude/commands/handoff.md` step 1 (`Capture reasoning that should survive this session. Record only durable facts`); the managed `.gitignore` header adds `generated, never committed`, though the blueprint is authored, not generated. The mid-session decision verbs of `/implement-task` and `/implement-pack` write only to that chain. The routes off the disk are the handoff's `ESPALIER_MEMORY.md` step, scoped to harness decisions, and the operator-approved reflect promotion; neither is presented as where a project decision must go.
