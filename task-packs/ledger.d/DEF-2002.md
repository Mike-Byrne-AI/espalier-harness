By arithmetic, not measured: for a linear consumer the net ratio crosses the bound when the slowdown adds more than `(net_small + 1) / 2` ms to the per-call floor (a 30 ms per-call cost, a 20 ms net small sample and a 1.5x slowdown read net 5 -> 25 ms against a bound of 16). It can only red a linear pattern, never pass a quadratic one, so the `DEF-2000` rows cannot see it. Who is hurt: the maintainer reading a required cell on a shared runner whose per-call cost sits near the net sample (the `DEF-926` host), who gets a super-linear red on a linear walk. The same class as `DEF-2000` (samples taken back to back against a ratio), on the false-red side; filed rather than fixed in that lane because it needs its own measurement on a host with a large per-call cost, which the Windows self-host box cannot give (a per-call cost near 1 ms, and these arms skip there without SIGALRM).
