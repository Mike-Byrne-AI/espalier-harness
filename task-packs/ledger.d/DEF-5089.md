The fix batch's failure-mode review measured 60 (module, name) pairs that no visible reader takes (2026-11-08): `tools/cc/hooks/_speedbump.py`'s kill reading takes nine `checkout_sync` names (`ancestors_of`, `process_cwds`, `CWD_PID_CAP` among them), `tools/cc/hooks/post_write_check.py` four `_record_fragments` names, the SessionStart banner seven `checkout_sync` names, and `ledger_row.py` and `record_merge.py` about forty from the ledger modules; `espalier/cli.py::_kept_sibling_skew_lines` printed nothing for a kept copy lacking `ancestors_of`, `window_pid`, `session_log_fragments` or `ledger_lock`, and named the control `find_root`.
