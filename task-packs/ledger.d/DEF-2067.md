| `DEF-2067` | tools/cc/board.py::stopped_lines (the re-send at the ruling is not read); tools/cc/seat.py::DISPATCHER_TICK | **The re-send at the ruling of a job the dispatcher released off a stopped seat is not read from the board, so a parked job can be lost when its operator call is ruled.** The approved shape of `DEF-3038` (ruling `20261107T060117Z-air-5e86f5` (C)) has the dispatcher release a job an open operator call parks, ack the stop, and re-send the job at the ruling. The board's `stopped:` lines (`tools/cc/board.py::stopped_lines`) make the release read every tick, but once it is done the stop is closed and the released assign leaves `jobs:`, so the board keeps no link from the job to the call. When the dispatcher rules the call, often hours later from a window opened after a threshold handoff, nothing on the board or in the dispatcher brief names the job to re-send (the failure-mode review of `DEF-3038`'s lane, finding 6; the 11-08 hand application, `DEF-1071` parked on `17217c`, was carried in Session Log prose). Who is hurt: the worker seat whose parked job is never re-sent, and the dispatcher, whose `under two:` tail reads that seat short and fills the slot the re-send needs. A fix shape to weigh: a release whose text names the call (a `parked:` marker in the family of `stopped:`), the call's `asks:` row naming the job it parks, and a `re-send:` line under the tail from the ruling's ack until an assign of the same job dated after it appears. | minor | LOGIC_BUG | OPERATOR |
