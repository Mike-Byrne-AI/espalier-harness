| `DEF-5073` | tests/test_reinject_sync.py::TestMirrorCensusCoverage (the sync-script and identical-bytes censuses) | Neither mirror census can see a family that is transformed in transit and kept by hand: `test_every_tracked_sync_script_is_claimed_by_a_row` starts from a tracked sync script and `test_every_identical_tracked_pair_is_linked_by_a_row` from files with identical bytes, and a rewritten copy with no script has neither. A maintainer who adds such a copy -- a source re-indented into a shipped body and kept in step by hand, pinned at most by a parity test of its own -- declares no registry row and nothing reds; every reader of the registry (the pre-flight's mirror lines, the edit-time advisories, the census tests) then treats the source as unmirrored, and an edit to it lands with no prompt to carry it across. Fix shape: a third census read from the parity tests themselves (each test that compares two tracked paths names a registry row), or accept the gap and say so in `espalier/mirror_registry.py`'s docstring. | nit |
