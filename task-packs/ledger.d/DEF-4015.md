| `DEF-4015` | tools/cc/hooks/_reinject.py::check (one session slot per non-exempt fire; ::REINJECT_SESSION_CAP is 5); tools/cc/hooks/_reinject.py::VENDOR_SYNC_RULE, ::DOCS_ASSET_SYNC_RULE, ::CLAUDE_SURFACE_SYNC_RULE, ::TASK_PACKS_ROUTER_SYNC_RULE, ::SELFCHECK_MIRROR_SYNC_RULE, ::HARNESS_GUARD_SYNC_RULE, ::PACK_CHECKLIST_SYNC_RULE (none cap_exempt); tools/cc/hooks/session_start.py::_clean_state_flags (the counter survives compact and resume) | **The mirror-sync advisories share the recall engine's five-fire session budget with every other non-exempt row, so after five fires in a session the advisory root `CLAUDE.md` promises for every mirror row goes silent. On the Air, nearly four in five of their fires were suppressed.** Root `CLAUDE.md` (Architecture Rules) says: "A PostToolUse advisory names the right sync for whichever row you touched." `tools/cc/hooks/_reinject.py::REINJECT_SESSION_CAP` is 5 (line 51), and `::check` spends one flocked `reinject_count` slot per non-exempt emit and drops any non-exempt row past the cap as `session_capped`. None of the seven mirror-registry rows (`::VENDOR_SYNC_RULE`, `::DOCS_ASSET_SYNC_RULE`, `::CLAUDE_SURFACE_SYNC_RULE`, `::TASK_PACKS_ROUTER_SYNC_RULE`, `::SELFCHECK_MIRROR_SYNC_RULE`, `::HARNESS_GUARD_SYNC_RULE`, `::PACK_CHECKLIST_SYNC_RULE`) is `cap_exempt`. The vendor row fires on every Edit or Write under `tools/cc/`, so five hook edits spend the budget for the session. `tools/cc/hooks/session_start.py::_clean_state_flags` clears the counter only on startup and clear, so a compaction or a resume leaves it spent. Measured from the Air's self-host recall log (`.espalier-state/recall_events.jsonl`, gitignored; `_telemetry_enabled` keeps pytest runs out) from 2026-08-07 to 2026-10-12: the seven rows emitted 512 times and were session-capped 1,892 times. In 139 (row, session) pairs over 257 session keys, a mirror row was capped without having emitted under that key: the docs-asset row 54 times, the claude-surface and vendor rows 35 each. That is an upper bound, because the key moves at a compaction and the counter does not. Driven 2026-10-12 with the counter stubbed past the cap and every writer stubbed: an Edit to a source or generated path of six mirror rows returns no payload, and with the counter at 1 each returns one. Who: a maintainer past their fifth hook edit, or past a compaction, who edits a `.claude/` body, a deployed doc or the harness-guard asset gets no sync line (and on a generated mirror no "this edit will be DISCARDED" line), and meets the drift at proof time or in CI instead of at the edit. Whether the contract tier's generator `--check` run catches every row was not driven (probable). Fix shape (untested): take the mirror rows off the shared budget. Either make them `cap_exempt`, with the generated-side discard warning on every edit and the source-side reminder `once_per_session` per row, or give them a budget of their own. Either way, mind the documented invariant that more than `REINJECT_PER_TURN_CAP` exempt rows co-firing on one event starve the rest. Refuted if the root sentence is meant as best-effort and the cap is the intended trade; then the sentence is the fix. The probe imports the module, stubs the counter at 99 for `reinject_count` only and stubs telemetry and once-flags, so it writes nothing. It drives `check` on six mirror paths and prints `True` while any of them returns nothing. | minor |
