| `DEF-4116` | tests/test_ci_job_bounds.py::_SERIAL_JOBS_MEASURED_WORST (no test derives a census-basis bound from its entry) | **No test derives a census-basis CI bound from its `_SERIAL_JOBS_MEASURED_WORST` entry, so a bound raised from a newer census read can leave the dict behind with every test green.** `tests/test_ci_job_bounds.py::TestEveryJobThatRunsTheSerialFilesOutlastsTheirMarks::test_its_bound_holds_the_worst_mark_plus_its_measured_run` asserts each bound is at least the serial files' worst mark plus the entry, a floor: a bound raised with its entry left as it was still passes. `test_the_floor_is_the_mark_plus_the_worst_rounded_up_to_five` drives the census's `serial_floor` on literals, never on the dict. So the dict, whose comment says its figures are what each bound was last set from, can drift from the bounds with nothing red, and two entries left as ruled (`portability.yml` portability, `release.yml` release-check) are not what their bounds were set from (#614's residue, air-work; note 344b8f). Who is hurt: the seat that raises a bound next and reads the dict as its basis, taking a stale figure for the job's measured worst. Fix shape (probable): for each job whose bound the census sets by its serial floor, assert the bound equals `serial_floor` of its entry and the worst mark, and mark the entries another rule sets as such. The probe reads whether any test function in the file uses both the dict and the census's `serial_floor` (`False` while the defect stands); a fix in another shape repins it. | minor | HYGIENE | MAINTAINER |
