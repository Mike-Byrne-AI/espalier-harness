| `DEF-1141` | .claude/commands/implement-pack.md (step 12: stamps Commits: with step 10's SHA, then amends that commit) | **Step 12 of `/implement-pack` stamps the pack's `Commits:` line with step 10's SHA and then amends that commit, so wherever the move is committed the stamp names a commit no branch reaches.** The step fills `Commits:` with "the SHA from step 10", then lands the move "with the pack's commit" by `git commit --amend --no-edit -- <the paths this step changed>`, which writes a new commit under a new SHA. Where `Done/` is tracked (a repository that versions its packs, or an adopter tree after the `.gitignore` line doctor names is deleted), the stamped pack rides the amended commit, so re-stamping it means amending again and no stamp can name the commit that carries it; on this repository, where `Done/` is ignored and the move is a tracked deletion, the stamped copy stays local and names the pre-amend commit. `.claude/skills/blueprint-authoring/SKILL.md` asks for alive SHAs in that field. Who is hurt: an adopter or maintainer who later follows a landed pack's `Commits:` line to revert the pack or read its diff, and finds a SHA that no other clone resolves and this one resolves only until the reflog expires. Fix shape: where the stamped file is untracked, stamp after the amend; where it is tracked, land the stamp and the move as a commit of their own on top of step 10's instead of amending it, so the SHA it names stays the pack's commit; run `scripts/sync_claude_mirrors.py`.
