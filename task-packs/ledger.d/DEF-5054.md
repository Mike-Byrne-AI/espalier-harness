| `DEF-5054` | espalier/_rmtree.py::_make_handler (the retry calls func(path), and rmtree's os.open needs its flags) | **`espalier/_rmtree.py`'s refusal handler retries the call that failed as `func(path)`, and when that call was `shutil.rmtree`'s own `os.open` of a directory it cannot read, the retry raises `TypeError` (`open() missing required argument 'flags'`), which neither `best_effort=True` nor a caller's `except OSError` catches.** The handler adds the owner write bit and calls `func(path)` once more; for the unlink and rmdir calls that is the original call, but `rmtree`'s fd-based walk passes `os.open`, whose retry needs its flags. A tree holding a directory with no read bit (a test's `chmod 0o000`, or an adopter's locked folder) therefore escapes every best-effort delete as a `TypeError`. Measured 2026-11-01 on 3.14 (the probe), and by the code review of the temp-sweep lane on 3.13 and 3.10.21. Who is hurt: an adopter whose `espalier cleanup` or a `fuse` rollback meets such a directory, who gets a traceback where the module promises a reported refusal; and the suite's session-start sweep, which now isolates each entry so one such tree costs that entry alone. Fix shape: the handler retries through the call's own arguments (for `os.open`, re-raise the original `PermissionError` after the chmod, or retry with the flags `rmtree` uses), with a row in `tests/test_rmtree.py` that deletes a tree holding a 0o000 directory under `best_effort` on both spellings. | minor | LOGIC_BUG | ADOPTER |
