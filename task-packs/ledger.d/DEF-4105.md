| `DEF-4105` | .github/workflows/test.yml (the test, test-serial and perturbed jobs print --durations=25 with no ceiling beside it) | **No CI run reports a test's time against its own timeout, so a test drifting toward its ceiling is found only when it crosses it on a slow cell and reds a pull request that never touched it.** The `test`, `test-serial` and `perturbed` jobs in `.github/workflows/test.yml` set `--durations=25` in `PYTEST_ADDOPTS`: the 25 slowest calls by wall time, with no ceiling beside them, so a test under a long mark can run at 59 of its 60 s and never make the list. `tests/_timeout_backstop.py` acts only once a ceiling has fired. win's census (note a3a2ce, 2026-11-08) found four tests at or above 0.75 of their ceiling, one at 59.3 of 60 s; DEF-2056 then gave four such sites measured ceilings one by one, after #519's and #516's reds, each of them one test (the sdist bench in `tests/test_wheel_payload.py`) crossing its 300 s timeout on slow cells. Who is hurt: a seat whose pull request draws a slow cell and reds on a test it never touched, and spends a cycle proving the red is not its own. Fix shape: each CI run reports every test's call time beside its own timeout (its pytest-timeout mark, else the ini default) and warns past 75% of it, seeded from win's census; where it runs (a pytest plugin in the test tree, or a step over the junit report) is the fix's choice.
