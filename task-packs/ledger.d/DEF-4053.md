| `DEF-4053` | tools/cc/seat.py::box_lock_default (and tests/_box_lock.py::BoxLock._log_wait) | **The box lock's default path, and the wait log beside it, live under the interpreter's temp directory, so two seats launched from contexts whose `TMPDIR` differ lock two files; and the log records waits alone, so an empty log cannot say whether no run waited or no run was gated.** `tools/cc/seat.py::box_lock_default` returns `os.path.join(tempfile.gettempdir(), BOX_LOCK_NAME)`; `child_env` sets `ESPALIER_BOX_LOCK` to it where the parent named none, and `command_line` exports it as `${ESPALIER_BOX_LOCK:-<default>}`, so a seat launched from a terminal and one launched from an app or a service with its own `TMPDIR` resolve different files (`tempfile.gettempdir` reads `TMPDIR` first), and the per-user directory it falls back to is swept by the host (air-work's reading). `tests/_box_lock.py::BoxLock._log_wait` is called from `_acquire` alone, after a wait that polled at least once (`last_report is not None`), and appends one line to `<lock path>.log`; a run that took the lock at once, or passed through inside its holder, writes nothing, so the record `TP-703c`'s re-measure reads (its line 151: whether a flag is built is the wait log's to say) holds waits alone. Who is hurt: the operator whose two seats on one box launch from contexts with different `TMPDIR`, whose two tiers then run beside each other on 8 GB with the lock on and nothing said; and the operator reading an empty `<lock path>.log` for the re-measure, who cannot tell no waits from no gated runs. Found by `TP-703c`'s reviews; reported by air-work 2026-10-26 (note 20261026T024838Z-air-work-5a5eba, item 1) for air's block; verified against the tree 2026-10-26 (the default's parent is `gettempdir()`; `_log_wait` has the one caller, behind the wait). Fix shape, one lane: a durable per-user home that reads no `TMPDIR` (`Path.home() / ".espalier"`, the precedent `tools/cc/hooks/_integrity.py` sets for its audit log), the log recording every gated run's outcome (taken at once, waited, passed through, unguarded) so an empty log is readable, `tests/test_seat.py`'s path pins and `tests/test_box_lock.py`'s log case moved with it. The probe resolves `box_lock_default` in two child interpreters whose `TMPDIR` differ and prints 1 while the two paths differ (a home under `gettempdir()` with another name would still read open); the log half has no oracle of its own and closes with the path. | minor | HYGIENE | OPERATOR |
