| `DEF-5012` | tools/cc/hooks/write_guard.py::_adopter_zone (an adopter zone holding harness ground gets the relaunch) | **An adopter whose own protected zone holds harness ground (`tools/` listed, `tools/cc/` inside it) is told, on a move or delete of that zone, to relaunch in maintenance mode or relocate, and following either takes the harness's hooks with it.** `tools/cc/hooks/write_guard.py::_adopter_zone` keeps harness precedence by design (DEF-995's constraint): a target that is, sits in or encloses a harness zone or file gets the harness text. For `mv tools x` with `protected_paths = ["tools/"]` that is "Bash move of protected harness zone blocked: tools" with the maintenance relaunch and the relocate line (driven by the failure-mode review of lane/adopter-zone-deny-one-liners, 2026-10-16); before that lane it read "This zone is YOURS" under the adopter's own template, whose Don't refused the relaunch. Following the relaunch turns every zone check off and the move carries `tools/cc/hooks/` away; the relocate line, done from a terminal, does the same to the next session; the right remedy (move your own files out of the zone, leave the harness's in place) is in no template. Who is hurt: an adopter who lists a directory holding harness ground and moves or deletes it. Fix shape: a deny for an operand that encloses harness ground inside an adopter zone names both (the zone the adopter wrote and the harness ground it holds) and offers neither the relaunch nor the relocate, in a template of its own. The probe drives the move on a temp adopter tree and reads whether the relaunch is offered. | minor | LOGIC_BUG | ADOPTER |
