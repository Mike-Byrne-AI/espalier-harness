Espalier deploys nothing under `.claude/hooks/`, so a native guard there can be rewritten to exit 0, or a helper it imports can be broken through a Bash write, with no Espalier friction; only the wiring in `settings.json` is protected. Found by the 2026-10-03 adoption audit (`espalier-issues/audits/2026-10-03-adoption/public_report.md`, B-4); re-checked at `08ab2db`: LIVE. Nothing got worse at install; this is a missing opportunity, a sibling of the unseeded `protected_paths` seam. The plan-relief half, that no setting can make `plan_guard` require a plan under an exempt prefix, is DEF-952's; this row is the remainder, deriving protection from the wiring. Who is hurt: an adopter whose own guard hooks live in `.claude/hooks/` and who assumes the harness that sits beside them also keeps them from being edited away.
