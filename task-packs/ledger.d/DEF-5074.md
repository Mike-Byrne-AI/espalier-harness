| `DEF-5074` | tests/test_reinject_sync.py::TestMirrorCensusCoverage::test_no_generated_side_advisory_prescribes_its_own_destruction (the has_sync skip) | The generated-side advisory census skips every unscripted row: `test_no_generated_side_advisory_prescribes_its_own_destruction` opens its loop with `if not r.has_sync: continue`, and its pre-flight twin `test_surface_impact_generated_side_also_never_prescribes_its_own_destruction` does the same, so the edit-time advisory of a `sync=None` row is held to nothing. The pre-flight side is held since `DEF-477` (`_surface_impact_gaps` requires an unscripted row's mirror side to say copy by hand); the hook side is not. A maintainer who declares a hand-copied family and writes its advisory from a sibling rule's text can ship a message naming an unrelated sync script; the reader runs it, it exits 0, and they rule the drift's cause out. No `sync=None` row exists today. Fix shape: hold an unscripted row's mirror-side advisory to `mirror_registry.remedy`'s copy-by-hand line and to naming no sync script, as `_surface_impact_gaps` does for the pre-flight. | nit |
