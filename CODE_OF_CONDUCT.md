# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant v2.1** as its Code of
Conduct. The full canonical text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
and applies in full to every interaction in this project's spaces
(issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
official-channel communication).

## Reporting

Instances of behavior that violate the Code of Conduct may be
reported privately to the project maintainer at
**mbbyrne.byrne@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. All reporters' identities and
the details of any incident will be kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing the
standards of acceptable behavior described in the Contributor
Covenant. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful, per the Covenant's
"Enforcement Guidelines" section.
